Orion v0.3.0 — Orion gets a look of its own.

Everything here is visual. Your data, workspaces, resources, scripts and timers are untouched, and nothing needs to be re-done after updating.

## Four themes

Settings → General now has a theme picker.

- **Graphite** — the look Orion has always had, and still the default. Updating does not change how your app looks.
- **Deep field** — cool near-black, warm ink, soft blue.
- **Ember** — warm near-black, serif titles, hairline rules instead of boxes.
- **Almanac** — monospaced and achromatic; fits the most on screen.

The choice applies everywhere, including Quick Launch and the timer alert, and it travels with your backups.

## Every workspace has a constellation

The small coloured dot beside each workspace is now a figure drawn from that workspace itself — the same one every time, so you come to recognise a workspace by its shape rather than by reading down the list.

The figure brightens with the time you have logged against it, measured against your own busiest workspace rather than a fixed number, so it stays meaningful whether you have twenty hours in Orion or two thousand. A new workspace reads as faint but deliberate, never broken.

The colour you picked still shows: it tints the figure.

Renaming a workspace keeps its constellation.

## A running pomodoro is visible without looking for it

A bar along the top edge of the window fills as the session runs. The timer still shows the exact numbers; the bar answers "am I nearly done" without you having to find anything on screen.

Stopwatch sessions have no bar, because there is no end to be near.

## Smaller things

- Switching workspace and summoning Quick Launch now settle rather than snap. Everything is under a fifth of a second and nothing waits on an animation; if you have reduced motion turned on in Windows, Orion honours it.
- Text across the three new themes is measurably more legible than in the drafts — every text colour was checked against the WCAG AA contrast standard rather than judged by eye.
- Resetting a workspace's total time now updates the sidebar immediately instead of only the panel you reset it from.
